## Interpretation and decision

Observed value is **selective**: a generic review instruction elicited the missing mandate inquiry, a concrete missing attribution component, and more specific collateral/FX/basis questions without exposing the answer key. It also repeated covered issues, failed to fully address the six unresolved compound targets and missed both known unsupported conclusions. This supports usefulness of a second pass in these particular records, with critical assessment of its objections, rather than comprehensive detection or a trusted approval layer.

This tests **independent execution and review instructions with the same underlying model, not independent expertise**. There is no matched single-model self-review control, no revised answer/outcome evaluation and no separation of extra reading, prompt framing, stochasticity and execution time effects. Six synthetic variants of one portfolio, one review each, narrative cues, non-blinded/non-independent assessment and judgment-sensitive compound criteria do not establish reliability, causality or multi-agent superiority. Case 05's original spread-direction wording conflicts with the rubric and reviewer framing; that unresolved input problem is preserved, not silently repaired. Selected zero shocks versus stress wording elsewhere also remain limitations.

**Decision:** retain the observed examples as evidence of limited additional review value; do not integrate a reviewer into Metis. No further inference, experiment or framework is initiated.
