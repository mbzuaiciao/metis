# Metis: Meta hackathon retrospective

**Evidence reviewed through November 5, 2026.** This retrospective covers the hackathon workstation and associated research, using main at `8d4ccfe` and explicitly identified research-branch records. It synthesizes existing evidence; no new inference, experiment or infrastructure evaluation was performed. The [research log](../research/RESEARCH_LOG.md) indexes the detailed reports, including failures and dated corrections. Scenario dates are fictional or historical economic labels, not development dates or evidence of hackathon eligibility.

## Purpose and original hypothesis

Metis explores a portfolio manager's investigation workflow: what happened, what might explain it, what could happen under different assumptions, and what evidence is still needed? The working hypothesis was that deterministic financial attribution plus structured model interpretation could make that investigation more useful and inspectable. The goal was financially sound explanation with explicit uncertainty, rather than fluent commentary alone. This is the research objective recorded in the [research log](../research/RESEARCH_LOG.md#a-research-objectives), not an established advantage over a bare LLM.

The main lesson is that correct calculations, valid structured output and useful economic synthesis are separate achievements. Metis demonstrated all three in selected examples, but also demonstrated that they can coexist with misleading attribution, unsupported conclusions and missed questions. No study establishes general financial reliability, real-portfolio performance or identified causal effects.

## Experiments: what changed our understanding

The following are selected studies, not a pooled benchmark or a complete inference census. Most use `muse-spark-1.3`, temperature 0.2, an 8,000-token completion budget and manual financial review. Low effort applies only where specified in the linked protocol. Hosted weights are unpinned; small, purposive synthetic samples and non-blinded review limit generalization. Detailed reports retain settings, counts, usage and unavailable metadata.

| Study and evidence | Recorded observation | Lesson and resulting decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Full Context vs Evidence Only](../research/financial-reasoning-ablation.md), two opposite-order pairs, **4 live calls** | Removing five narrative fields preserved the central synthesis in these samples. Both conditions still made financial errors: HY net/spread labeling, overlapping DV01, first-order terminology or contribution-versus-total reasoning. | Useful synthesis did not require those particular narratives. This is not a bare-LLM comparison: calculations, schema, prompt cues and curated history remained. No overall winner; stop after the replication. |
| [Guided liquidity mechanisms](../research/liquidity-causal-mechanisms.md), **1 call**, and [unguided probe](../research/unguided-causal-reasoning.md), **1 separate call** | Guided output explained supplied safety-buying and forced-selling channels. The leakage-audited unguided response also volunteered both, but an unsupported cash-buffer characterization remained. | Mechanisms can be articulated without explicit naming in one completion. Different contexts prevent a controlled prompting-effect claim; spontaneous discovery and causal identification remain open. |
| [Observer/revision study](../research/observer-experiment.md), **10 cases, 40 unique calls** | Cases with material financial errors fell from 3 originals to 1 in each revision condition. Two material issues were repaired; another error and important omissions survived. All three objections were accepted. | Correction is possible, but extra revision/feedback calls are confounded and bad-advice rejection was not shown. Same-model calls are not independent expertise. Do not add an always-on observer. |
| [Self-review comparison](../research/self-review-vs-observer.md), **10 new attempts, 9 valid**, reusing observer archives | Matched-nine self-review and observer-assisted revisions repaired the same liquidity issue and retained the same material miss. The missing self-review case timed out after 60.056s; the archived observer had repaired it. | Matched-nine additional stages used **98,013 vs 160,169 reported tokens**, with similar latency. No full-ten equivalence or monetary saving; neither workflow justified trusted integration. |
| [Grounding instructions](../research/evidence-grounded-reasoning.md), **10 fresh cases, 20 attempts** | Baseline 8 valid/2 truncated; added rules 6 valid/4 truncated; only five complete pairs. Helpful qualifications coexisted with unsupported conclusions and signed-FX errors. | More instructions did not demonstrate net financial improvement. Keep baseline; incomplete pairs constrain comparison. |
| [Compact output](../research/inference-reliability.md), **4 pairs**, plus **3 live workflow checks** | Both paired conditions completed 4/4. Compact responses serialized 32.7% smaller while completion tokens rose 5.9%; unsupported financial language persisted. | Fewer visible words are not a measured reliability improvement. Keep baseline. Successful demos did not erase the separate truncation evidence. |
| [Low reasoning effort](../research/reasoning-effort-experiment-20261029.md), **4 pairs / 8 calls** | Both conditions completed 4/4. Low effort used 3,903 vs 20,508 reasoning tokens; summed request latency was 39.096s vs 85.069s. The dated correction gives **51,743 vs 69,422 total tokens**, replacing the original erroneous split. | Increased observed headroom supported the narrow configuration change. Neither condition failed here, so this does not measure a failure-rate reduction or prove truncation eliminated. Provider internals and general quality remain unknown. |
| [Offline meaning alignment](../research/meaning-alignment.md), **16 authored statements, 0 inference calls** | Rules flagged 7 of 8 intended errors, abstained on an unfamiliar paraphrase, and preserved supported/conditional examples. Rules were written with cases visible. | Semantic scope matters beyond correct numbers, but this is in-sample rule coverage, not general language understanding. Keep research-only. |

Two later findings make the limits especially concrete:

**Historical specificity did not establish better mechanism reasoning.** The [September 2022 macro-context pair](../research/historical-macro-context-20220913.md) used two successful low-effort requests, **12,825 total tokens / 29.986s summed request latency**. Adding BLS/Reuters context made the answer more event-specific, but the financial-only answer already proposed the main policy, growth and dollar channels. No substantive incremental mechanism benefit was demonstrated. Added detail also included a core-inflation surprise not supported by the supplied headline consensus. The subsequent [single product-integration call](../research/historical-hypothesis-implementation-20261103.md) demonstrated three hypotheses with portfolio implications and proposed checks, with wording limitations; it did not validate their causes.

The historical calculation itself evolved transparently. Original research omitted unknown spreads and reported **−$1,416,477.20 partial-factor P&L** on synthetic holdings. A later [credit correction](../research/historical-credit-pnl-correction-20261103.md) sourced IG 0bp/HY +18bp, explicitly mapped US indices to the baskets, and added **−$80,100**, giving **−$1,496,577.20** across the four modeled factors. European credit uses cross-region proxies; rates use a parallel 10Y proxy despite a different observed 2Y move. This was a sourced numerical correction with **no new inference**, not an improvement in historical model reasoning or an actual portfolio backtest. [Historical tests](../tests/test_historical.py) preserve old captured partial contexts and check new mapping/sign/reconciliation behavior.

**Correct selected mappings missed the actual question failure.** The [November 4 three-question diagnostic](../research/live-meta-preset-questions-20261104.md) completed three low-effort calls, **41,312 tokens / 79.161s route latency**. All nine selected mappings were Verified. Yet position ranking omitted important individual net losses, and the regime comparison substituted an inflation selloff for the requested rally and reversed the supplied equity-delta sign. The [offline trace](../research/preset-ranking-trace-20261104.md) found the intended question and complete position evidence reached the provider; relevant fields were partly buried in disclosures. Prompt competition is a hypothesis, not a proven cause. The [post-guidance evaluation](../research/answer-first-preset-evaluation-20261104.md) attempted two requests, both returning cannot-reach-Meta errors: zero completed answers, upstream receipt/tokens/cost unavailable. Visibility regression checks passed; live financial improvement could not be assessed.

## Infrastructure evaluation: Meta, Modal and the Baseten evidence gap

**Meta hosted API:** the shipped workstation uses a small HTTP adapter rather than a serving platform or general agent framework. Recorded live successes establish that path worked for selected Muse Spark requests. Visible failures, sanitized diagnostics and explicit LIVE META / DETERMINISTIC DEMO labels preserve the distinction between network inference and fixed templates. This architecture kept financial truth in the backend and made provider errors observable. It does not establish that hosted inference is cheaper or better than self-hosting. [Harness](../backend/metis/reasoning/harness.py), [truncation diagnosis](../research/meta-inference-truncation-diagnosis.md).

**Modal:** evidence extends beyond main's proposed backlog. The unmerged `research/omission-probe-20261101` branch contains a completed infrastructure/training pilot, closed November 2 at [`edf9104`](https://github.com/mbzuaiciao/metis/commit/edf9104). Its [branch research log](https://github.com/mbzuaiciao/metis/blob/edf91043d0aba3dff5500117e25d81338a92d970/research/RESEARCH_LOG.md) and [final report](https://github.com/mbzuaiciao/metis/blob/edf91043d0aba3dff5500117e25d81338a92d970/research/knowledge_application_20261101/FINAL_REPORT.md) are historical branch evidence, not shipped application capabilities.

The log records failed setup attempts, corrections to SDK/server resource assumptions and a technical smoke pass. The pilot used pinned `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507` revision `cdbee75f17c01a7cc42f958dc650907174af0554`, an L40S and Transformers 4.56.2. Intended greedy settings initially resolved to sampling until model-default handling was corrected. One intentional LoRA training submission returned an adapter after **60 examples, 3 epochs and 45 recorded optimizer steps**. Training completion and finite tensors did not establish financial learning.

In the final v4 comparison, base/stronger-prompt arms completed 80/80 responses; the adapter arm completed four and truncated on its fifth call at 512 tokens without EOS. Of 120 scheduled slots, **84 completed, 1 truncated and 35 were unattempted**; no complete adapter knowledge/application pair existed. The no-repeat intervention was selected after observing a held-out failure, so v4 was a repair study, not untouched confirmation. **Financial performance was unassessed; the comparison remained incomplete and no production integration followed.**

This pilot taught operational lessons: inspect effective decoding rather than intended settings, preserve termination evidence, and separate technical completion from financial scoring. Modal's available call graph could not certify complete physical-attempt history; `retries=0` did not establish exactly-once execution. Actual attributable charges and billable GPU time remained unknown. The final **$15 planning allowance against a $20 target was not a provider bill or enforced cloud cap**. Those limits prevent a defensible hosting-cost comparison with Meta.

**Baseten:** no verifiable Baseten experiment, deployment, benchmark or outcome was found in the reviewed tracked files, available Git history or matching issue/PR search results. This is an evidence gap, not proof that no external exploration occurred. The retrospective cannot reconstruct a Baseten-versus-Modal selection decision or claim comparative quality, latency or cost without records.

## Lessons, remaining limitations and research questions

1. **Make evidence scope part of the architecture.** Captured portfolio/date/shocks, original calculations, proxy labels and visible errors made mistakes diagnosable. Snapshot preservation fixed context drift; it did not solve semantics. The cache is bounded, in-process and FIFO; browser refresh/restart is not durable conversation persistence. [Scope implementation](../backend/metis/reasoning/followup.py), [scope audit and qualifications](../research/investigation-scope-audit.md).
2. **Evaluate the answer's requested distinction, not only its selected numbers.** Net versus factor P&L, positive rates contribution versus total profit, signed exposure versus mark, and cash versus contractual liquidity repeatedly separated plausible prose from supported conclusions. Model-selected checks leave omissions outside coverage. [Verification report](../research/financial-claim-verification.md), [live question review](../research/live-meta-preset-questions-20261104.md).
3. **Preserve negative results and corrections.** Observer repairs, self-review timeouts, grounding truncations, corrected effort-token totals and the incomplete Modal pilot all change the practical decision. Pooling them into one success rate would mix different cases, protocols, dependencies and missingness. Detailed reports and append-only corrections are more useful than a single headline score.
4. **Treat causal scenarios as conditional investigations.** Analyst-accepted shocks let the engine compare consequences reproducibly. Neither economic fluency nor sourced event reporting establishes a dominant cause. Historical quotes are asynchronous and retrieved-vintage; synthetic holdings, proxy mappings and illustrative magnitudes limit realism. [Causal study](../research/causal-exploration.md), [valuation-coverage stopping decision](../research/instrument-valuation-coverage-20261103.md).

The financial engine omits convexity, curve/key-rate risk, default jumps, carry, funding, liquidity, margin, transaction costs and dynamic sensitivities. The local workstation has no production authentication, durable storage, trading, optimizer or real-time ingestion. Cached observations and illustrative reconstructions do not amount to evaluation on real portfolios. Mocked transport tests verify contracts; demo/browser checks verify workflows; neither measures live model quality. See the [README limitations](../README.md#validation-and-limitations).

**Open questions, not established benefits or authorization for new work:** Does the harness improve interpretation over a matched bare-model baseline? Can held-out semantic checks catch wrong scope and omitted claims while abstaining appropriately? Can self-review or a different observer reject bad advice at justified total cost? Which macro inputs add discriminating predictions beyond event recognition? Can causal proposals specify evidence and thresholds that separate competing mechanisms? Can a complete, prospectively frozen smaller-model/training evaluation improve missing-evidence recognition without termination regressions? What would an audited infrastructure comparison show once cold starts, effective settings, physical-attempt uncertainty and actual charges are measured? Existing studies motivate these questions; none settles them.
