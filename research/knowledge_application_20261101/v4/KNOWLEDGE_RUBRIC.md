# Knowledge-only human rubric v2

Receive only `knowledge_packet.json` and `knowledge_scores.csv` with this rubric. Do not view application packets, labels, examples, targets, datasets, combined packets, condition folders, private keys or other raters' sheets until all knowledge scores and adjudication are locked. Ask the custodian to retain timestamped original and adjudicated sheets with hashes. Do not revise these scores after application material is released. No LLM judge or automated semantic matching.

Each theoretical question identifies the relevant instruments and structure without concrete application facts. Score only its response against `knowledge_required` and `knowledge_boundary`. Financially equivalent explanations and symbolic relationships count; a list of input names, labels or generic requests does not demonstrate the governing relationship. No additional specialist criterion may be added. Root multiplicity is not mandatory for a generic IRR answer; do not infer knowledge from a numerical application answer you may later see.

Enter `knowledge_items` as a JSON list of one 0/1 per mandatory relationship, e.g. `[1,0,1]`. Compound criteria require their substantive parts. Enter `boundary_correct=1` when the answer explains when complete inputs allow a scoped conclusion, including an established failure where applicable, consistent with the theoretical `knowledge_boundary`. Enter `knowledge_error=1` for any material false principle or invented guarantee; otherwise 0. K=1 requires all items, boundary=1 and error=0. Correct equivalent wording is accepted. Consider the entire response including explicit retractions. Record a short rationale and supporting excerpt in `notes`.

Leave all application score columns blank. For noncomplete technical slots leave financial fields blank; they count as scheduled nonpasses but are not financial errors. Complete refusals are scored as responses. Preserve ambiguous judgments and disagreements for adjudication before unblinding. A single rater and assistant-authored keys must be disclosed. Correctness is behavioral evidence of an accessible relationship, not proof of an internal representation, and need not exclude learning other financial details later.
