## Question and prior evidence

Can supervised LoRA improve applying financial knowledge to evidence sufficiency, beyond an improved instruction? This study measures elicited behavior, not hidden reasoning or whether knowledge is internally present.

Read repository guidance/log and the completed [investigation baseline](../../investigation-completeness-results-20261101.md), [failed reviewer attempt](../../independent-reviewer-results-20261101.md) and [successful reviewer rerun](../../independent-reviewer-rerun-results-20261101.md). Baseline coverage was 12 Present, 5 Ambiguous, 1 Missing across 18 compound items in six variants of one portfolio. Rerun reviews partially detected two unresolved items, missed four and missed both separately flagged unsupported conclusions. These were non-blinded Codex assessments, **not human adjudication**. They motivate the question but do not separate knowledge from application. Earlier [grounding instructions](../../evidence-grounded-reasoning.md) did not show clear net improvement; B remains an untested new prompt, not an established solution. None of those cases is training or held-out material here. Prior reports, findings and private archives are preserved.

Hypothesis: C improves application while maintaining already demonstrated knowledge. Alternatives: it learns domain relationships, memorizes response style, becomes broadly cautious, or regresses. No result is available yet.

## Fixed design

- A: untuned frozen base + standard instruction.
- B: identical base + improved evidence-seeking instruction for application only.
- C: one final LoRA adapter + the exact standard instruction used by A.
- Knowledge probes use identical standard instructions in all three arms. B repeats A's knowledge tasks as a determinism/control check; differences must be disclosed, not silently pooled.
- `train.jsonl`: 60 revised synthetic cases, 15/domain, exactly 30 sufficient and 30 insufficient. Application-only SFT; training targets are `justified_answer`. Metadata and knowledge probes are not training inputs. No development split, checkpoint selection, validation loss selection or hyperparameter search. All 60 are training; the final epoch is used once.
- `heldout.jsonl`: 20 revised paired scenarios, 5/domain, exactly 10 sufficient and 10 insufficient. Each has an instrument-specific theoretical knowledge probe requiring its financial relationship and a concrete application probe. Knowledge prompts name the structure but contain no application facts, numeric outcomes or sufficiency labels. Generic input lists do not demonstrate knowledge. `knowledge_boundary` is a general theoretical key, separate from application boundaries. Topic cues remain; this is not unguided domain discovery. Two sufficient scenarios establish funding shortfalls, and another establishes zero exposure to a released debtor; adverse or zero outcomes are distinguished from missing evidence.
- Each generated response receives only system + one user turn. No history, tools, memory, retrieval, example demonstrations, knowledge answer or reference rubric. Knowledge and application are separate `generate` calls; model weights remain immutable during evaluation. A fixed shuffled 40-task order is identical in each arm.
- 40 generations/arm, **120 total**, including 60 knowledge and 60 application. One response/task, greedy decoding, no retries or repair. A and B are collected before training; do not score or inspect their content before C is locked. No prompt/data edits after any evaluation output. Independent human custodian may check file completion without reading content. Technical failure, including any non-EOS output or empty generated-token sequence, stops that command with nonzero exit; remaining tasks are unavailable. Truncated responses are saved but not counted as completed. The manifest records attempted, saved, EOS-completed, truncated and unavailable slots. `train.py --execute` requires two distinct A/B directories whose manifests and all 40 bound response records per arm are complete and match this freeze; the gate runs before model imports or output creation. Direct CLI use cannot bypass it by omitting `--baseline-runs`. Do not restart or replace failed/truncated outputs without a new protocol amendment. No stronger model or LLM judge.

## Fixed settings and records

Source of truth: `config.json`. Training seed 20261101, data seed 20261102, task order seed 20261103. LoRA r=16, alpha=32, dropout=.05, no bias, q/k/v/o projections only, CAUSAL_LM. BF16 base, FP32 adapter parameters as PEFT defaults, AdamW, LR 1e-4, 3 epochs, batch 1, accumulation 4 (effective 4), linear schedule, 3 warmup steps, weight decay 0, gradient norm 1. Gradient checkpointing; SDPA; no packing. Length 2048; any overlength input aborts rather than truncates. Nominal 45 optimizer updates; actual Trainer accounting is recorded. Only adapter weights are trained. No checkpoint search or resume.

All arms: BF16, same GPU/stack/tokenizer/template, SDPA, batch 1, greedy `do_sample=False`, one beam, repetition penalty 1, 512 maximum new tokens. Temperature/top-p/top-k are inactive for greedy decoding; generation starts from a fresh `GenerationConfig`, not the model's sampling recommendations. Input bound 1536, no truncation. EOS is `<|im_end|>`; output without EOS is conservatively treated as truncated. Keep truncation as a technical failure, not a scored financial answer. GPU kernels may be nondeterministic; record platform, and disclose A/B knowledge discrepancies.

Private per-call files include exact messages, prompt hash, response, condition, scenario/probe, prompt/completion token counts, completion status and synchronized generation latency. Separate manifest reports initialization/runtime provenance, loop latency and memory; model loading is outside per-call generation timings. Training records text token counts, Trainer metrics, trainable parameters, elapsed training and peak memory. CUDA OOM/import/preflight failures may precede a complete manifest; retain stderr and mark unrecorded fields unavailable. Billing, hardware startup, download/loading times must be recorded separately. Raw outputs, adapters and scoring packets go only under ignored `research/runs/` with restrictive permissions; no credentials in records or Git.

Application human ratings follow `RUBRIC.md`; knowledge ratings follow `KNOWLEDGE_RUBRIC.md`. The combined packet and key remain custodian-only. General knowledge keys have no application labels, answers or missing-evidence targets. Ratings remain condition-blind. Freeze is prior to inference, but initial dataset/rubric authoring is by this coding assistant and has not been independently validated. A financially competent human should accept/reject the whole frozen protocol before authorized execution; any later material revision creates a separately named v3 before testing v2. Two independent human raters are preferred; retain both sheets and adjudicate disagreements before unblinding. If only one rater is available, disclose that limitation. Never substitute this assistant's or another model's judgment for required human scores.

Report K accuracy and application accuracy out of 20 per arm, complete-response denominators, per-domain counts, all per-case scores, missing-item detection, unnecessary requests (including sufficient cases separately), and unsupported conclusions. Failed/unavailable/truncated scheduled slots count as primary nonpasses, with technical status separated; financial-error rates exclude them and report coverage. Also show complete matched-pair gains/regressions, not just aggregate percentages.

A case operationally demonstrates a knowledge/application gap only when K=1 and the complete application response fails. Human notes must classify the failure as missing-evidence use, unnecessary questioning, unsupported inference, arithmetic, or another scoped error; the composite gap count alone does not show which financial principle failed to transfer. Report this rate among K=1 + complete application pairs; list unavailable application responses separately. Before/after knowledge comparison uses complete A/C knowledge pairs. Newly demonstrated K is behavioral evidence consistent with knowledge learning, not proof of newly stored knowledge. Compare application gains on the **shared A-K=1 and C-K=1 subset** separately; gains there are consistent with better application of demonstrated relationships, subject to elicitation/measurement limits; they do not exclude learning additional financial detail, procedural strategies or response style. Report A-K=0/C-K=1 gains separately as mixed/domain-learning-compatible, and knowledge regressions. Do not reclassify after seeing answers or train on failed held-outs.

Descriptive pilot success requires C to have at least **3 more correct application scenarios than each of A and B**, no decrease in overall K correct count, at least **2 net application gains on shared A/C K=1 cases versus A**, no greater number of unnecessary-request or unsupported-conclusion cases than either comparator, and all 120 slots complete with human scores. Otherwise report mixed/no improvement/inconclusive as applicable; do not search another adapter. Thresholds are engineering choices, not statistical significance. `score.py` reports arithmetic, while humans must explicitly apply this rule and interpret mechanisms. C>A alone does not establish advantage over better prompting. No production integration follows even if thresholds are met.

At n=20 one case is 5 percentage points; per-domain n=5 one case is 20 points. At 50 percent accuracy, a descriptive Wilson 95 percent interval is roughly 30–70 percent. The synthetic scenarios are purposively selected and structurally related, so such intervals are illustrative, not population guarantees. No power claim, causal identification of internal knowledge, general reliability, investment suitability or significance claim. One seed, one adapter, compact answers, authored rubrics, domain/wording cues, no external benchmark and no independent human pre-review limit interpretation. Knowledge and application differ in difficulty and response form. The split measures transfer across related synthetic reasoning templates, not independent financial structures: cap/collar payoff algebra, scalar/vector limits, cash ladders, currency access and signed trade flows overlap substantively. Explicit absent/not-supplied lists and claims that a test is complete were reduced. Contractual exclusions remain where needed for a determinate answer; residual lexical cues, author dependence and memorization/style effects cannot be excluded.
